Named explicitly, because a gap you cannot see is a gap you fill from memory:

- **Raid attackers, Dynamax/Gigantamax tiers, regionals, never-transfer lists** — moved to
  `META-PVE.md`. That file is hand-maintained and unverified; treat every number in it as a
  hypothesis. Raid counters and regionals are *generated in the app itself* from
  `src/data/raid-bosses.json` and `src/data/regional-forms.json`, which are the real sources.
- **Per-species rank-1 IV spreads.** The filters above use one loose rank-1 shape
  (atk 0-1, def 3-4, HP 3-4) for the whole pool. For an exact per-species spread, use a PvP
  IV checker — this snapshot does not carry one.
- **XL-candy requirements and legacy/Elite-TM move gating.** Real collection criteria, not
  yet automated. See `docs/gbl-collection-research.md` in the repo.
- **Tournament (Play! Pokémon) legality.** Not machine-readable; mythicals and box legendaries
  are banned there but legal in casual GBL, so the two lists differ.
